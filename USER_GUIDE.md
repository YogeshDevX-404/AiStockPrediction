## PART 2 — GETTING STARTED

Follow these simple steps to get started:

### Step 1: Open the Application & Create an Account
1. Open TradeGenius AI in your web browser.
2. Click **Get Started** or **Register** on the top right corner of the landing page.
3. Enter your full name, email address, and desired password.
4. Click **Create Account**.

### Step 2: Logging In
1. If you already have an account, click **Login**.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

### Step 3: What Happens After Login?
Once logged in, you will be taken directly to the **Main Dashboard**. From here, you can view market summaries, top gaining stocks, AI recommendations, your watchlist, and your current portfolio status.

### Step 4: How to Navigate
* **Left Navigation Menu (or Top Bar on Mobile):** Use the sidebar to jump between **Dashboard**, **Portfolio**, **Watchlist**, **AI Signals (Predictions)**, **Market News**, **AI Assistant**, and **Settings**.
* **Global Search Bar:** Use the top search bar at any time to type a stock ticker symbol (e.g., `NVDA`, `AAPL`, `MSFT`, `TSLA`) and press Enter to open that company's detail page.

## PART 4 — MARKETS & STOCK EXPLORATION

### How to Find & Research a Stock
1. **Search for a Stock:** Click the search icon or bar at the top of the app, type the ticker symbol (e.g., `NVDA` for NVIDIA) or company name, and select it from the dropdown list.
2. **Understand Price & Change:** 
   * **Current Price:** The latest price at which the stock traded.
   * **Daily Change:** Green indicates the stock price increased today (`+$4.42 / +3.45%`), while Red indicates a price drop.
3. **Understand Charts:**
   * **Line Chart:** Shows the simplified path of the stock price over time.
   * **Candlestick Chart:** Displays opening, closing, highest, and lowest prices for each time interval.
   * **Timeframes:** Toggle between **1D** (1 Day), **1W** (1 Week), **1M** (1 Month), **1Y** (1 Year), or **5Y** (5 Years).
4. **Understand Volume:** Indicates how many shares were bought and sold today. High volume means strong market interest.
5. **Workflow for Finding a Trade Idea:**

$$\text{Search Stock} \longrightarrow \text{Open Stock Page} \longrightarrow \text{Review Price \& Fundamentals} \longrightarrow \text{Check AI Signal \& Confidence} \longrightarrow \text{Check News} \longrightarrow \text{Add to Watchlist or Trade}$$

## PART 5 — STOCK DETAIL PAGE

When you open an individual stock (e.g., `NVDA`), you get access to 11 detailed sections:

1. **Header & Quick Actions:** Shows company name, stock exchange (e.g., NASDAQ), sector, and quick buttons to **Add to Watchlist** or **Paper Trade**.
2. **Live Price Card:** Displays real-time price, daily high/low range, opening price, previous close, and trading volume.
3. **Interactive Trading Chart:** Allows you to analyze chart trends using technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages).
4. **AI Recommendation Card:** Displays the AI's conviction signal (**Strong Buy, Buy, Hold, Sell, Strong Sell**), confidence score (e.g., `88%`), target price, and main technical drivers.
5. **Key Statistics Grid:**
   * **Market Cap:** Total value of all company shares. *(Why care? Tells you if the company is a massive stable mega-cap or a smaller volatile company).*
   * **P/E Ratio (Price-to-Earnings):** Measures stock valuation. *(Why care? Higher P/E can mean high growth expectations or overvaluation).*
   * **EPS (Earnings Per Share):** Net profit per share. *(Why care? Higher EPS indicates strong profitability).*
   * **Dividend Yield:** Percentage of stock price returned to shareholders annually as cash dividends. *(Why care? Great for passive income investors).*
   * **Beta:** Volatility relative to the overall market. *(Why care? Beta > 1 means the stock swings more wildly than average).*
6. **Technical Indicators:** Shows real-time momentum scores like **RSI** (Relative Strength Index: >70 is Overbought, <30 is Oversold) and **MACD** trend crossovers.
7. **Financial Statements & Quarterly Results:** Tabs for **Income Statement**, **Balance Sheet**, **Cash Flow**, and **Quarterly Revenue/Profit Growth**.
8. **Shareholding Pattern:** Breakdown of ownership between Promoters, Foreign Institutional Investors (FII), Domestic Institutions (DII), and Retail investors.
9. **Company Profile & Dividend History:** Company background, CEO name, founded year, employee count, website link, and past dividend payouts.
10. **Analyst Ratings:** Aggregated price targets and rating breakdown from Wall Street research firms.
11. **Related Peers & News Feed:** Recent news stories specifically about this stock, along with direct comparisons to competitor companies.

## PART 6 — AI PREDICTION

### What is AI Prediction?
The AI Prediction engine processes market data, volume spikes, chart patterns, and financial statements to forecast potential future stock movements.

### Key AI Metrics Explained
* **Bullish:** The AI expects the stock price to rise.
* **Bearish:** The AI expects the stock price to fall.
* **Neutral:** The AI expects the stock price to move sideways without a clear direction.
* **Conviction Rating (Strong Buy / Buy / Hold / Sell / Strong Sell):** The overall recommended action based on combined technical and fundamental signals.
* **Confidence Score (e.g., 85%):** How probability-certain the AI model is about its prediction based on historical pattern matches.
* **Target Price:** The estimated future price level projected by the AI model.
* **Prediction Timeframe:** The target duration (e.g., Short-term 1 Week, Medium-term 1 Month, or Long-term 3 Months).

### Important Disclaimer
> [!IMPORTANT]
> AI predictions are probability-based decision-support tools. They are **NOT guaranteed financial advice** and do **NOT guarantee profit**. Stock markets involve risk, and unexpected market events can change stock directions at any time.

### Realistic Example of Using AI Predictions
> *Scenario:* You search for **NVIDIA (NVDA)**. You see an AI signal of **Strong Buy** with an **88% Confidence Score** and a **Target Price of \$155.00** (current price \$132.40). 
> 
> *What you should do:* Do not buy blindly! Check the technical indicators on the stock chart, read recent company news, verify quarterly revenue growth in financial statements, and if satisfied, add NVDA to your watchlist or execute a paper trade.

## PART 7 — WATCHLIST

### What is a Watchlist?
A Watchlist is a personalized list of stocks that you want to track without buying them immediately.

### Why Create One?
* Monitor potential buying opportunities before committing cash.
* Compare daily performance across multiple stocks side-by-side.
* Stay updated on price breakouts for your favorite companies.

### How to Use the Watchlist Page
1. **Add a Stock:** Click **Add Ticker**, type the ticker symbol, and click **Save**.
2. **Remove a Stock:** Click the trash icon next to any ticker in your table.
3. **Compare Tickers:** Click **Compare Tickers** to view a side-by-side table of price changes, P/E ratios, and AI scores.
4. **AI Watchlist Radar:** Automatically highlights which of your watched stocks have sudden momentum breakouts or unusual volume spikes today.

## PART 8 — PORTFOLIO

### What is Portfolio Management?
The Portfolio page allows you to track all your real or practice stock holdings in one place.

### Key Metrics Explained
* **Total Value:** Current market value of all your stock holdings combined.
* **Total Profit/Loss (\$ and %):** Total gain or loss made since purchasing your stocks.
* **Daily Change:** Total money gained or lost today.
* **Dividend Yield:** Total passive annual income expected from your dividend-paying stocks.

### How to Add & Track Holdings
1. Click **Add Holding**.
2. Select or enter the stock ticker (e.g., `AAPL`), enter the **number of shares** owned, and the **average price paid per share**.
3. Click **Save Holding**.
4. The platform will automatically calculate your real-time profit/loss, asset allocation by sector, and portfolio health score.

## PART 9 — ALERTS & NOTIFICATIONS

### What are Smart Alerts?
Smart Alerts notify you when a stock meets specific conditions so you don't have to watch charts all day.

### Supported Alert Types
* **Price Alert:** Notifies you when a stock price goes above or below a target dollar amount (e.g., *"Alert me if NVDA goes above \$140"*).
* **Technical Indicator Alert:** Triggers when indicators break key thresholds (e.g., *"Alert when RSI falls below 30 - Oversold"*).
* **AI Signal Alert:** Triggers when the AI prediction changes (e.g., *"Alert when AI signal flips from Hold to Strong Buy"*).
* **Breaking News Alert:** Sends notifications when major news impacts your watched stocks.

### How to Create an Alert
1. Go to the **Alerts** page or click **Alert Manager** on any stock page.
2. Click **Create Smart Alert Rule**.
3. Choose the stock ticker, condition type, target threshold, and notification preference (In-app, Browser Push, or Email).
4. Click **Create Rule**.

## PART 10 — MARKET NEWS & SENTIMENT

### What is the News Section?
The News section aggregates real-time market headlines from major financial news outlets and automatically runs Artificial Intelligence NLP (Natural Language Processing) analysis to score each article's sentiment.

### Understanding Sentiment Scores
* 🟢 **Bullish Tag:** News containing positive company growth, strong earnings reports, or strategic upgrades.
* 🔴 **Bearish Tag:** News containing negative reports, earnings misses, lawsuits, or regulatory risks.
* ⚪ **Neutral Tag:** General market commentary or routine updates.

### How to Use News for Investment Decisions
Never make an investment based on a single news headline. Combine news sentiment with chart trends and AI prediction scores for a balanced view.
